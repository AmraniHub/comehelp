Delete your ComeHelp account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open ComeHelp and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap Delete my account and data, then Yes.

Without the app

Email privacy@amrania.com from any address with the subject "Delete my ComeHelp account", giving the phone number you used and your group's name. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone number and place: erased from the group and from every alert you sent.
  • Your "I'm coming" answers and this phone's notification link.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Alerts you sent stay in the group's history without your name or number, and are deleted automatically after 12 months.
  • Reports about abuse are kept up to 12 months to protect the group, then deleted.

If you were your group's only admin, the longest-standing member becomes admin; a group with no members left is closed.
