How ComeHelp works

1. Join or create a group

Join: open the invitation link a member sent you: it opens ComeHelp, or Google Play, and the code fills itself in. No link? Open ComeHelp → I have a code → type the 6 letters, or tap Paste the code after copying the message. Your group decides who gets in: an admin, or members who know you.
Create: tap Create a group, give it a name (your village or street) and you become its admin. In the Group tab, share the invite link, show the QR code at a meeting, or add people you know: each gets a one-time code to send by WhatsApp or SMS. Every member can invite.
Several groups: your street and your parents' village, say. Tap the group's name at the top → Join or create another group (up to 5). Alerts from every group ring; in Me you can set a far-away group to a plain notification.
New phone? Type your recovery code (Me → Recovery code) in I have a code: all your groups come back. Lost it? Ask your admin for a personal code (Group → Add someone you know, with your number).

2. Make sure your phone rings

Open Me. The checklist shows every setting that can stop an alarm, each with a Fix button. Then tap Ring my phone in 10 seconds, lock the screen, and wait.

Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO: also turn on Autostart, Show on lock screen and Display pop-up windows while running in the background for ComeHelp (the checklist opens the right screens). After an update, check them again.

3. Send an alert

  1. Alert tab → choose what is happening.
  2. Check the pin on the map; if it is not on the right spot, tap Correct the spot.
  3. Add a short note if you can (no names).
  4. Press and hold SOS for 3 seconds.

Every member's phone rings with the place on a map. Without internet, ComeHelp texts the alert to the members instead (one normal SMS each).

4. Answer an alert

Tap I'm coming (the sender sees your name) or I can't; both stop the siren on your phone. To stop it without answering, tap Silence or press a volume button, as for a phone call. The sender, or an admin, taps It's over when it is over, and the siren stops on every phone.

On a map inside a page, move and zoom with two fingers; one finger scrolls the page. Tap the map to open it full screen.

5. Practise without disturbing anyone

Tap Practise first under the SOS button (or Me → Practise an alert). Everything works as for real: choose the danger, hold SOS, record your 10 seconds. Then only your phone rings, marked 🎓 Practice, so you can try I'm coming, Silence and the volume button. Nothing reaches your group and nothing is kept; your recording stays on your phone. Tap Stop practising to go back to real alerts.

For admins

  • Requests: accept or decline straight from the notification, or in the Group tab. Only accept people you know.
  • Who gets in: the admins decide; or members who know the person vouch for them (1 to 3 of them, and a single ⚠ leaves it to the admins); or anyone with the code, for a family or a small trusted group.
  • Drill: rings every phone with a test, and shows which ones did not ring.
  • Members: make someone admin, or remove them.

ComeHelp alerts your neighbours. It does not replace emergency services: in real danger, call your local emergency number first.
