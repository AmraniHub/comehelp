Your neighbours with you in a minute

Theft, fire, a medical emergency, a missing person, lost livestock, a flood: one alert rings every phone in your group, even on silent, with the place on a map. You see who is coming.

📲 Get ComeHelp for Android

ComeHelp is in review on Google Play. Until it appears there, install it from here:

Download ComeHelp (Android, 1.7 MB)How it works

  1. Open the downloaded file and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser when Android asks.
  2. Open ComeHelp → I have a code → type the code you received.

Xiaomi / Redmi: if it says "App not installed", open the file from the Files app instead of the browser. When ComeHelp is on Google Play, uninstall this copy and install it from there.

🔔 It really rings

A full-screen alarm with a siren on the alarm volume, even when the phone is silent or locked, and the alert read aloud.

📍 The place on a map

A satellite map with the spot of the danger and your position, and directions in one tap. The sender can move the pin.

🏃 Who is coming

Neighbours answer "I'm coming"; everyone sees who is on the way. The sender closes the alert when it is over.

📩 Works without internet

With no data, the alert goes out by SMS to every member, and their app still rings.

👥 Your own group

Anyone can create a free group for a village or a street. The admin invites people or approves requests.

🌍 Four languages

Arabic, French, English and Spanish, chosen in the app. Each member hears the alarm in their own language.

Important

ComeHelp alerts your neighbours. It does not replace emergency services: in real danger, call your local emergency number first.
